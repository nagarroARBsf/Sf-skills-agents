# Salesforce reference diagrams (picture references)

All 25 diagrams in Salesforce's Reference Architecture Gallery, plus the three platform variants of the Zero-Copy diagram (28 images), (https://architect.salesforce.com/diagrams),
in Salesforce's five types, downloaded as published at original size. They show how Salesforce's own
architects apply the Salesforce Diagrams standard. Before drawing a type, open two or three of that
type with the Read tool and match their structure. Each row links Salesforce's page
(`https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/reference-diagrams/guide/<file name>`).

These images are Salesforce material, included as references for drawing; they are not templates to
paste into client documents. Salesforce publishes them without a stated licence (see `NOTICE`).

## What every one of them has

- **Header band**: white panel with a curved right edge on a light-grey band; title in navy, regular
  weight; description in navy, two to four lines; optional logo top-left; the **key** on the right as
  a white rounded panel, in even rows of icon + label (or line sample + label).
- **Cards**: white, rounded, grey outline, faint shadow; optional icon (white glyph on a coloured
  circle) left of the title; a light-grey rule under the title; attributes as a plain list; optional
  grey footer band with a centred capability name; a centred row of footer icons.
- **Containers**: the same card, larger, holding child cards in even rows; nested up to three deep.
- **Connectors**: elbow lines, mostly straight; capsule pills with bold text; arrowheads small and solid.

## What the generator reproduces

Header band and key, card anatomy, containers with attributes, footer band and footer icons, dash-dot
boundaries, elbow connectors with pills, publish/subscribe and native/custom line styles, numbered
process steps, participant-column process flows, org-chart trees, roadmap time grids with spans and
lanes, release-plan stages, the data model notation.

Not reproduced (draw it by hand in draw.io when a client asks): colour-by-owner lines, latency clock
badges on lines, dark-background themes, layer bands with a coloured label column.
